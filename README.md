# My Favorite Series: Breaking Bad

I have been watching **Breaking Bad** recently, and it is arguably one of the best shows ever made. 

### Why I like it:
* **The Writing:** The character arc of Walter White is incredible.
* **The Acting:** Bryan Cranston and Aaron Paul have amazing chemistry.
* **The Tension:** Every episode feels like a movie.

> "I am the one who knocks!" - Walter White
